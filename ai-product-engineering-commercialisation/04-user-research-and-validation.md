# 04 — User Research & Validation

## Core Principle

> AI products should be validated by customer problems and measurable outcomes, not by technical excitement.

## Why User Research Matters

Many AI products fail because teams validate whether they can build something, instead of validating whether customers need it.

The correct sequence:

```
Customer Problem

↓

User Understanding

↓

AI Opportunity

↓

Prototype

↓

Measured Validation
```

## Research Areas

### Understanding the User

Identify:

- who experiences the problem;
- how often it occurs;
- current solutions;
- cost of the problem;
- willingness to pay.

### Problem Interviews

Strong interviews focus on:

- past behaviour;
- existing frustrations;
- current workflows;
- business impact.

Avoid asking only:

> Would you use an AI product?

People often like AI ideas but do not pay for them.

## AI-Specific Validation

AI products require additional validation:

- output quality;
- trust;
- accuracy expectations;
- human review requirements;
- acceptable automation level.

## SEMLIS Example

Instead of asking:

> Would sellers use AI?

Validate:

- How much time is spent analysing listings?
- How often are pricing decisions wrong?
- What profit is lost due to poor decisions?
- Would better recommendations create measurable value?

## Validation Loop

```
Hypothesis

↓

Customer Evidence

↓

Prototype

↓

User Feedback

↓

Improved Product
```
