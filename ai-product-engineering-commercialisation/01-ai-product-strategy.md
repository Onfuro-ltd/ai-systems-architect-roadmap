# AI Product Strategy

## Core Principle

Great AI products begin with valuable problems, not impressive technology.

AI should be applied where it creates measurable improvement:

- saves time;
- reduces cost;
- increases revenue;
- improves decisions;
- creates new capabilities.

## AI Product Framework

```
Customer Problem

        ↓

Value Opportunity

        ↓

AI Capability

        ↓

Product Experience

        ↓

Business Outcome
```

## AI-Native vs AI-Enhanced Products

### AI-Native

The product could not realistically exist without AI.

Examples:

- autonomous assistants;
- intelligent decision systems;
- personalised experiences.

### AI-Enhanced

An existing workflow becomes significantly better through AI.

Examples:

- customer support automation;
- analytics assistance;
- content generation.

## Problem Selection

Strong AI opportunities usually have:

- repeated workflows;
- large amounts of data;
- expensive human effort;
- clear success metrics;
- customers willing to pay.

## Product Strategy Questions

Before building:

1. Who has this problem?
2. How painful is the problem?
3. What is the current solution?
4. Why is AI better?
5. How will success be measured?
6. Who pays?
