# 05 — Approvals and Progressive Autonomy

## Purpose

Increase automation without silently removing meaningful human authority.

## Autonomy ladder

```text
Inform
  ↓
Recommend
  ↓
Draft
  ↓
Execute with approval
  ↓
Execute within bounded policy
  ↓
Broader autonomy
```

Movement upward requires evidence.

## Approval design

Show what will happen, target/destination, material values/content, source evidence, uncertainty and reversibility as relevant.

## Approval fatigue

If users approve hundreds of low-information prompts, oversight becomes ceremonial. Aggregate safe actions or automate proven low-risk cases while preserving review for meaningful exceptions.

## Policy boundaries

Even user approval should not bypass deterministic organizational restrictions.

## Reversibility

Prefer drafts, previews, staging and undo where possible.

## Delegated autonomy

Users can grant bounded authority by task, amount, destination, time, data scope or other explicit constraints.

## Exercise

Design a progression from recommendation to bounded autonomous execution for a recurring operational workflow.

## Takeaway

> Autonomy should be earned by measured reliability and bounded by explicit authority.
