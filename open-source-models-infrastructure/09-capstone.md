# Open Source Models & AI Infrastructure Capstone

## Purpose

This capstone combines model selection, hosting, adaptation, data, evaluation and enterprise operations into one complete AI infrastructure architecture.

## Reference Architecture

```
Business Applications

        ↓

AI Platform Layer

        ↓

Model Router

        ↓

+-------------------------------+
| Frontier Models                |
| Private Models                 |
| Fine-tuned Models              |
| Specialist Models              |
+-------------------------------+

        ↓

Knowledge + Tools + Data Systems

        ↓

Evaluation + Monitoring + Governance
```

## Core Principles

- Do not choose models before understanding the workload.
- Use retrieval and tools for changing knowledge.
- Use fine-tuning for specialised behaviour.
- Evaluate systems against business outcomes.
- Treat AI infrastructure as a production platform.

## Enterprise AI Strategy

The strongest systems will usually be hybrid:

- frontier models for complex reasoning;
- private models for sensitive workloads;
- specialist models for high-volume tasks;
- business rules for reliability.

## SEMLIS Relevance

A future commerce intelligence platform should combine:

- marketplace data;
- proprietary business workflows;
- specialised models;
- AI agents;
- evaluation feedback loops.

The long-term advantage is not owning the largest model. It is building the strongest intelligence system around a domain.
