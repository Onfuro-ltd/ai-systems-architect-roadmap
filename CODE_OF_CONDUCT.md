# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project welcomes engineers, researchers, founders and learners who want to improve understanding of AI systems.

Contributors should:

- communicate respectfully
- focus on evidence and reasoning
- welcome different technical perspectives
- avoid hype and misinformation
- share knowledge constructively

## Technical discussions

Disagreement is expected. The goal is to improve decisions through analysis, experiments and evidence.

Critique ideas, architectures and implementations, not people.
