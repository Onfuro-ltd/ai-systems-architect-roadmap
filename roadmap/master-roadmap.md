# AI Systems Architect Roadmap

## Vision

A roadmap for founders, engineers and technical leaders who want to design, build and operate AI-native systems.

## Core principle

Models will change. Durable advantage comes from architecture, knowledge, data, tools, evaluation and governance.

12. AI system design
13. Data architecture
14. Open-source and local AI
15. Fine-tuning and specialist models
16. AI infrastructure
17. Multimodal AI
18. Computer-use agents
19. Enterprise AI
20. AI-native business systems

The roadmap evolves as the AI ecosystem changes.
