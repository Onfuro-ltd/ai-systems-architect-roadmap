# AI Product Engineering & Commercialisation v1.0

## Purpose

This section bridges AI technology and successful AI businesses.

Building an AI system is only one part of creating value. Enterprise AI products require:

- customer understanding;
- product strategy;
- scalable architecture;
- business models;
- distribution;
- measurable outcomes.

## Core Principle

> The best AI product is not the one with the most advanced technology. It is the one that solves a valuable problem better than existing alternatives.
