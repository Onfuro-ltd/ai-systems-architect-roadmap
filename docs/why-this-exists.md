# Why This Exists

AI is advancing faster than traditional education paths can adapt.

Many resources focus on individual tools:

- chat assistants
- frameworks
- prompts
- agent libraries
- model releases

This roadmap takes a different approach.

The goal is to understand how complete AI systems are designed and operated.

## The Problem

A tool-first approach creates short-term knowledge that becomes outdated quickly.

A systems approach creates durable understanding.

## The Goal

Move from:

"Using AI"

To:

"Designing AI-native systems."

## Intended Audience

- founders building AI companies
- engineers building AI products
- architects designing enterprise systems
- leaders making AI strategy decisions

## Long-Term Vision

Create an open knowledge base for the AI systems era.
