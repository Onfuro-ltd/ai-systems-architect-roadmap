# Image Generation

## Overview

Image generation systems create visual content from prompts, references, or structured instructions. They are powerful creative tools, but they should be understood as generation systems rather than replacements for design, product knowledge, or quality control.

## Core Principle

> Image generation creates possibilities. Business systems decide what is usable.

A reliable workflow combines:

- generation;
- brand rules;
- product truth;
- human review;
- compliance checks.

## How Modern Image Generation Works

High-level pipeline:

```
Prompt / Reference Image

        ↓

Visual Representation

        ↓

Generative Model

        ↓

Generated Image

        ↓

Validation

        ↓

Approved Asset
```

## Genuine Business Value

Useful applications include:

### Product Marketing

- lifestyle scenes;
- background generation;
- campaign concepts;
- creative variations.

### Design Exploration

- prototypes;
- packaging ideas;
- visual concepts.

### Content Production

- social media assets;
- advertisements;
- presentation visuals.

## Current Limitations

Generation systems still require validation for:

- product accuracy;
- dimensions;
- branding;
- text rendering;
- legal requirements;
- misleading representation.
