# 06 — Audio & Speech

## Core Principle

> Audio AI is not only transcription. It is perception, understanding, generation, and interaction.

Modern audio systems combine:

- speech recognition;
- language understanding;
- voice generation;
- speaker and acoustic analysis;
- real-time interaction.

---

## Audio AI Pipeline

```
Audio Input

↓

Speech / Sound Processing

↓

Transcription or Representation

↓

Reasoning

↓

Response / Action
```

---

## Speech Recognition

Converts audio into structured information.

Examples:

- customer calls;
- meetings;
- interviews;
- voice commands.

Enterprise systems require:

- accuracy measurement;
- language support;
- privacy controls;
- human review where needed.

---

## Voice Generation

AI can generate natural speech for:

- assistants;
- training systems;
- customer communication;
- accessibility tools.

However:

- identity protection matters;
- consent is required;
- generated voices need governance.

---

## Real-Time Voice Agents

A production voice agent requires more than speech generation.

```
User Voice

↓

Speech Recognition

↓

Agent Reasoning

↓

Tools / Knowledge

↓

Voice Response
```
