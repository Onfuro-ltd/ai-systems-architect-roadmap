# Video Generation

## Core Principle

Video generation is not simply image generation repeated over time. A production video system must maintain temporal consistency, controllability, editing capability, and business relevance.

## Video Generation Pipeline

```
Creative Goal

↓

Storyboard / Planning

↓

Generation Model

↓

Temporal Consistency Checks

↓

Editing / Refinement

↓

Publishing Workflow
```

## Genuine Capabilities

Video AI is creating real value in:

- marketing content;
- product demonstrations;
- training material;
- simulations;
- localisation;
- rapid creative testing.

## Current Limitations

Important challenges:

- inconsistent objects across frames;
- inaccurate physical behaviour;
- text rendering issues;
- brand inconsistency;
- copyright and ownership questions;
- need for human review.

## Enterprise Architecture

Weak:

```
Prompt
 ↓
Video
 ↓
Publish
```

Strong:

```
Business Objective

↓

Verified Product Knowledge

↓

Video Generation

↓

Brand + Compliance Checks

↓

Approval

↓

Distribution
```

## Commerce Applications

Relevant examples:

- product demonstrations;
- marketplace videos;
- TikTok Shop content;
- advertising variations;
- customer education.

## Key Lesson

AI video should be treated as a production capability inside a larger system, not as a replacement for creative strategy and quality control.
