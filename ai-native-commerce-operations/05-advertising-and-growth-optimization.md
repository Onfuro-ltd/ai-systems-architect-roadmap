# 05 — Advertising and Growth Optimization

## Purpose

Optimize advertising against incremental commercial outcomes rather than isolated platform metrics.

## Inputs

Combine campaign/search data with product economics, stock, price, catalogue quality, seasonality and downstream sales outcomes.

## Metrics

CTR, CPC, conversion, ROAS and ACOS are useful diagnostics but not complete business objectives.

## Contribution

A campaign can generate revenue while destroying contribution after product, fulfilment, fees, returns and advertising costs.

## Incrementality

Attributed sales are not necessarily sales caused by advertising. Use experiments or appropriate causal methods when incremental effect matters.

## Decision loop

```text
Campaign + product signals
       ↓
Opportunity / problem detection
       ↓
Diagnosis
       ↓
Budget / bid / keyword / listing proposal
       ↓
Constraints + approval
       ↓
Execution
       ↓
Incremental / commercial outcome
```

## Coupling

Do not aggressively advertise products that are unavailable, uncompetitive, poor quality or structurally unprofitable without an explicit strategic reason.

## Exercise

Design an advertising recommendation that considers contribution, stock cover and catalogue quality alongside ad metrics.

## Takeaway

> Growth optimization should maximize valuable incremental demand, not merely platform-attributed revenue.
