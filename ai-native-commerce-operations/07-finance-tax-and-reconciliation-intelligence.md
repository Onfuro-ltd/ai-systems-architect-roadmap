# 07 — Finance, Tax and Reconciliation Intelligence

## Purpose

Use AI to investigate and explain financial operations while keeping accounting and tax logic controlled and auditable.

## Financial truth

Maintain orders, settlements, fees, refunds, taxes, payouts, invoices and ledger mappings with source identities and reconciliation status.

## Deterministic calculations

Tax rates, arithmetic, accounting mappings and known fee formulas should be deterministic and versioned where rules are expressible.

## AI role

AI can classify unexplained transactions, interpret documents, investigate discrepancies, summarize evidence and propose mappings or actions.

## Reconciliation

```text
Expected commercial events
        ↕
Channel / payment / fulfilment records
        ↕
Financial ledger
        ↓
Matched / exception / unresolved
```

## Temporal rules

Tax and fee rules can change. Preserve effective dates and jurisdiction/source evidence.

## Human authority

Material accounting adjustments, filings and uncertain tax treatments require appropriate qualified review and authorization.

## Outcomes

Measure unmatched value, ageing, duplicate/missing transactions, close time, adjustment rate and verified financial accuracy.

## Exercise

Design a settlement-reconciliation workflow where AI investigates exceptions but cannot silently alter the ledger.

## Takeaway

> Financial AI should make discrepancies easier to understand; it should not make financial truth probabilistic.
